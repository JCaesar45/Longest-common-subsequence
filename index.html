Frequently Asked

Where does my data live? +

Encrypted at rest in regional vaults. No third-party model providers receive identifiable request payloads.

Can I self-host the identity service? +

House members receive the Java identity container and deployment playbooks for on-premise or private cloud attestation.

What makes this different from a virtual assistant? +

AETHER agents coordinate across services, reason about constraints, and maintain persistent context rather than executing one-off commands.
